# Brag Plan: Namma Nyaya

## What is this app?
A GenAI legal companion for Bengaluru migrant tech workers that reads a
rental agreement or offer letter, explains it in plain English or Kannada,
flags the clauses that usually cause trouble, and backs every answer with
the actual law behind it.

## The angle
This isn't a generic "AI legal chatbot" — it's built for one very specific
moment: a newcomer to Bengaluru, PDF in hand, about to sign something they
don't fully understand. The video should feel like watching that moment get
defused — upload, red flag caught, explained in the language you actually
think in — rendered in a warm, paper-like, trustworthy editorial style that
looks like a document itself, not a SaaS dashboard.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The real hero headline slams onto the cream page in the serif display face:
"Understand your rental agreement before you sign it." — no logo yet, just
the sentence every migrant renter in Bengaluru has needed. Cream background,
warm ink-black type, doing all the work.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A rental agreement PDF gets dropped onto the upload card; the document
  pane fills with real clause text, and a red-flag card slides in labeled
  "SECURITY DEPOSIT — high" with its excerpt and law citation.
- The EN/KN language toggle gets clicked and the explanation panel's text
  switches from English to Kannada script (ನಮ್ಮ ನ್ಯಾಯ) — proving this isn't
  a translated afterthought, Kannada is a first-class output.

## Outro / punchline
The wordmark locks: "Namma Nyaya" over "ನಮ್ಮ ನ್ಯಾಯ", with the product's own
disclaimer line doing the punchline honestly: "Information, not advice. But
information you can finally read." — earns trust by being self-aware rather
than oversold.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result, pulled straight from the document page:
1. **Entry:** drop/select a rental agreement PDF on the upload card.
2. **Key action:** the backend analyzes it — clause text appears, a red flag
   surfaces with severity + citation.
3. **Result:** toggle to Kannada — the same explanation re-renders in
   ನಮ್ಮ ನ್ಯಾಯ's native language, proving the bilingual promise.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, premium civic-tech film — a legal document
  brought to life, not a startup pitch
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, confident restraint. Motion is
  smooth and deliberate (crossfades/slides, no hard cuts or flashes).
  Typography carries the video; color stays warm and paper-toned rather than
  glossy-tech.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18-19 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#f5f3ee` (warm cream), cards on `#fffdf9`
- Accent: `#8c2f27` (red-flag brick red) for alerts; `#2f6b46` (muted green) for trust/ok states
- Text: `#1d1b17` (ink black), secondary `#4f4a41`
- Display font: Source Serif 4 (headlines)
- Body font: Public Sans; mono accents in IBM Plex Mono for labels/badges
- Kannada font: Noto Sans Kannada — used for the bilingual wordmark and the KN toggle result
- Strongest visual element: the bilingual wordmark "Namma Nyaya / ನಮ್ಮ ನ್ಯಾಯ"
  paired with the red-flag card (brick-red left border, severity badge,
  citation line) — both are real UI, not invented graphics.

## Share copy (draft)
"Built an AI that reads your Bengaluru rental agreement before you sign it
— red flags, plain English or Kannada, with the actual law cited. Namma
Nyaya, a hackathon build."

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents over a steady, clean bed
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (109.96 BPM, steady/clean — matches polished/cinematic)
- Music treatment: enters under the hook at low volume (~0.3), stays
  unobtrusive through the flow, small swell into the outro lock, fade out
  over the last ~1s
- Music cue guidance: preset read from bundled cues (`vol-12`). Target
  strong cues 8.74s (red-flag card lands), 13.11s (language toggle click),
  17.47s/18.56s (wordmark lock in outro). Beat grid available for any minor
  secondary accents but not needed — polished tone stays restrained.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the cream background/card panel may
  gain a faint warmth/presence breathe with RMS. No waveform/equalizer
  visuals, no pulsing text.
- SFX posture: minimal — 2-3 cues total. One soft drop for the red-flag
  card landing, one clean switch/click for the language toggle, one gentle
  bell/soft-impact for the wordmark lock. Nothing sharp or dense.
- Audio-coupled moments: red-flag card arrival (soft drop), language toggle
  click (switch sound), wordmark lock (soft bell).
- Restraint rule: no dense SFX, no aggressive hits, no beat-quantized text
  reveals — this is a trust product, the audio should feel calm and exact,
  not hyped.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3.0s
Cream (`#f5f3ee`) full-bleed background. The real hero headline sets in
Source Serif 4, ink-black, large: "Understand your rental agreement before
you sign it." No chrome, no logo yet — just the sentence, centered, settling
fully before scene 2 begins.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: calm confidence, invites attention without hype
Audio-coupled idea: none — let the type carry the moment in silence apart from the music bed
Music: bed fades in under the headline, low (~0.3)
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Upload & red flag — 6.5s (3.0s–9.5s)
Recreate the real upload card (dashed border, upload icon, "Drop a rental
agreement or offer letter") on the cream page. A cursor drags in
`rental-agreement-hsr-layout.pdf`; the filename lands on the card. Cut to
the two-column document workspace: masked clause text fills the left panel,
then the red-flag card slides in on top — brick-red left border, "SECURITY
DEPOSIT" label, "high" severity badge, one-line excerpt, citation
underneath.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated drag-and-drop of the PDF onto the
upload card, then the red-flag card slides in as a single deliberate arrival
(not a list; one flag is the whole beat)
Audio intent: the analysis lands with quiet certainty — this is the "aha, it caught something real" moment
Audio-coupled idea: soft drop/thud sound as the red-flag card settles, timed to its arrival
Music: bed continues, aim the card's landing near the 8.74s strong cue
Transition mood: soft slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Bilingual explanation — 5.0s (9.5s–14.5s)
The explanation panel is on screen in English. The EN/KN language toggle
(pill-shaped, two segments) gets clicked — cursor tap on "KN" — and the
panel's text re-sets in Kannada script (Noto Sans Kannada), including the
brand's own Kannada name appearing as a small aside: ನಮ್ಮ ನ್ಯಾಯ. Hold long
enough to read the shift as real, not a flicker.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated click on the KN toggle segment,
text cross-dissolves from English to Kannada
Audio intent: a small, satisfying click of correctness — this feature
actually works, not a gimmick
Audio-coupled idea: clean toggle/switch sound at the tap, timed near the 13.11s strong cue
Music: bed steady, no swell yet
Transition mood: crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro / wordmark — 4.0s (14.5s–18.5s)
Cream background returns, calm and empty. The wordmark locks center frame:
"Namma Nyaya" in Source Serif 4 over "ನಮ್ಮ ನ್ಯಾಯ" in Noto Sans Kannada
beneath it. Below, the product's own disclaimer line sets in small type:
"Information, not advice. But information you can finally read." Hold on
the full lock for the last ~1.5s.
Sequential/interaction: none — one clean settle
Audio intent: warm, earned close — no fanfare, just quiet trust
Audio-coupled idea: single soft bell/impact at the moment the wordmark
fully locks, timed near the 17.47s/18.56s strong cues; music fades out
under the final hold
Music: gentle swell into the lock, then fade to silence by 18.5s
Transition mood: hold (end)

**Music mood for this video:** polished / steady, restrained
**Audio summary:** One clean, unhurried bed carries the whole video at low
volume; three sparse, motion-matched SFX (card drop, toggle click, outro
bell) mark the only three moments that need marking, aimed loosely at the
track's strongest nearby beats without ever rushing the copy.
